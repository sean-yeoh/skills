# Intake questions

Ask only the questions that the codebase scan did not answer. Ask them in one message, grouped as below. Show the scan findings first, so the user confirms or corrects them.

## Business and product

- Legal entity name, entity type (for example, Pty Ltd, LLC, Ltd, GmbH), and company or registration number if it must be shown.
- Trading or registered business name, if it is different from the legal entity name (for example, an Australian registered business name or a US "doing business as" name). Both names usually appear in the documents: "[TRADING_NAME] is a trading name of [LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]".
- Country, and state or territory, where the entity is established. Registered address.
- Postal address for mail and legal notices, if it is different from the registered address (for example, a PO box). Marketing emails to US recipients need a valid postal address (CAN-SPAM).
- Product name, and the URLs or apps that the documents cover.
- A short description of what the product does.
- Users: consumers, businesses, or both. Free, paid, or both.
- Approximate annual revenue band and number of users (some laws apply only above a threshold). The user may decline; then apply the stricter rule and mark it as an assumption.

## Users and locations

- Countries and regions where the product has users, or that marketing targets.
- Minimum age. Is the product directed to children, or likely to attract them?
- Languages that the documents need.

## Data practices (confirm the scan findings)

- Personal data collected, and where: sign-up, profile, payments, uploads, support, analytics, logs.
- Purposes of each use.
- Third-party services that receive personal data.
- Hosting and storage regions.
- Retention: how long each type of data is kept, and what happens on account deletion.
- Cookies and similar technologies: which are non-essential (analytics, advertising, session replay)?
- Marketing email or SMS: is it sent? On what consent?
- Does the business sell personal data, or share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising?
- Sensitive data: health, biometric, precise location, financial account, government ID, children's data, or other special categories?
- AI features: which providers? Is user content used to train or improve models (own or third-party)? Are there automated decisions that significantly affect users?

## Terms of service

- Paid plans: billing period, free trial, automatic renewal, how to cancel, refund policy, price-change notice.
- User-generated content: can users post public content? Is there moderation? Is a copyright (for example, DMCA) process needed?
- APIs or developer access: rate limits, key handling, separate API terms?
- Prohibited uses specific to the product.
- Service levels: is there an uptime commitment or SLA?
- Governing law, and the courts or arbitration for disputes. For US consumers: does the user want an arbitration clause and class action waiver?
- Liability cap preference (for example, fees paid in the last 12 months).

## Contacts and metadata

- Email for legal notices.
- Email for privacy requests (and a web form or toll-free number if a law requires one).
- Email for abuse or copyright reports, if there is user content.
- Contact phone number, if the business wants to publish one. It is optional unless a law requires one (for example, a toll-free number under the CCPA for businesses that are not online-only).
- Data Protection Officer, EU representative, or UK representative, if appointed.
- "Last updated" date and version number.
- How users are told about changes (email, in-app notice, banner), and how much notice before changes take effect.
